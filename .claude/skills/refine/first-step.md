# Step 1: formalizing the brief

Read this when the chain has only `step-0.md`. The obligations read this way are in `obligations.md`, under Step 1, against the brief and the tree.

Step 1 formalizes the brief rather than refining a step. It breaks the prose into items,
and each item traces to a statement in step-0, quoted in the step's Changes. It writes the
chain's plan for the first time.

It reads the brief against the tree: the specs of every part the brief touches.
- A statement the tree already says is absorbed, naming the claim.
- A statement that changes a landed claim makes a Reopened entry, with the statement as its
  authority.
- It never reads another chain's steps or plan, except in-flight Files lists.

The brief commits only to what it says. Anything you would add is an assumption or a
question, and it names the statement that raised it. The Files list holds only what the
brief itself places, and may be empty. Step 1 lands nothing, because nothing in a brief is
settled.

Step 1 always asks `q-validation`: "How will we validate that it works?" If the brief hints
at an answer, that hint becomes the recommended option, not the answer.
